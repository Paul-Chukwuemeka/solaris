# Solar Installation Cost in Nigeria: A Complete 2026 Breakdown

"How much does solar cost?" is the first question almost every prospect asks — and the honest answer is: it depends on your system size. A 2kVA backup system costs very differently from a 10kVA whole-home hybrid. This guide breaks down the actual component costs and gives real price ranges for the most common system sizes in Nigeria in 2026.

---

## What Makes Up a Solar System Cost?

Every solar installation has five major cost components:

1. **Solar panels** — generate power from sunlight
2. **Inverter/charger** — converts and manages power
3. **Battery bank** — stores power for use when panels aren't generating
4. **Charge controller** — regulates charging (built into most modern hybrid inverters)
5. **Installation labour, cabling, and accessories**

Each of these has its own price range, and the total varies significantly based on system size, battery chemistry, and brand choices.

## Factors That Affect Your Final Price

**FX rate:** Most solar equipment is imported. The naira/dollar rate at time of purchase significantly affects panel and battery prices.

**Location:** Installation in Lagos or Abuja often costs more than Enugu due to transport and logistics.

**Roof type:** Flat concrete roofs require different mounting hardware than pitched roofs, which can add ₦30,000 – ₦80,000 to installation cost.

**Brand choice:** A Victron-based system will cost 50–80% more than a Growatt equivalent for similar performance. Both are good — the choice depends on your reliability requirements and budget.

**Lithium vs gel:** Upgrading from gel to lithium adds ₦300,000 – ₦800,000 to system cost but saves money over 5–10 years. See our [lithium vs gel comparison guide](/blog/lithium-vs-gel-battery-nigeria).
