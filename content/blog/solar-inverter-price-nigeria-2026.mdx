# Solar Inverter Prices in Nigeria (2026 Guide)

The inverter is the brain of your solar system. It converts DC power from your panels and batteries into the AC power that runs your appliances — and in a hybrid system, it manages the switch between solar, battery, grid, and generator automatically. Here's a comprehensive look at what inverters cost in Nigeria in 2026.

---

## Types of Solar Inverter Available in Nigeria

### 1. Off-Grid Inverters
These run entirely on solar and battery power — no grid or generator connection. Best for areas with no DISCOM supply at all.

**Price range: ₦80,000 – ₦450,000** (1kVA – 10kVA)

### 2. Hybrid Inverters
The most popular choice for Nigerian homes. Hybrid inverters can use solar, battery, grid, and generator simultaneously, automatically switching to whichever source is available. When solar is generating excess power, it charges the battery; when the battery is full, it can optionally export to the grid.

**Price range: ₦180,000 – ₦1,200,000+** (3kVA – 20kVA)

### 3. Grid-Tie Inverters
These require an active grid connection to operate. Common in commercial installations in areas with relatively stable DISCOM supply. Without battery storage, they offer no backup during outages.

**Price range: ₦150,000 – ₦800,000** (3kVA – 20kVA)

## How to Choose the Right Inverter Size

The inverter capacity must match or exceed your **peak load** — the maximum wattage of all appliances that could run simultaneously.

**Quick sizing rule:**
- Total simultaneous load × 1.25 (safety margin) = minimum inverter VA rating

For example, if your simultaneous load is 2,400W:

**2,400W × 1.25 = 3,000VA → choose a 3kVA or 3.5kVA inverter**

For heavy loads like air conditioners, use a 2× surge factor:
- A 1.5HP AC draws ~1,100W running, but 3,300W at startup
- Your inverter must handle the surge
