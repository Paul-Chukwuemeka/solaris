# Lithium vs Gel Battery: Which Is Right for Your Solar System?

The battery is often the most expensive component of a solar system — and the one that most directly determines how long your power lasts. Choosing between lithium (LiFePO4) and AGM gel batteries is one of the most important decisions you'll make. Here's a clear, honest comparison for Nigerian conditions.

---

## The Two Main Battery Technologies

### AGM Gel (Sealed Lead-Acid)
AGM (Absorbent Glass Mat) gel batteries are the traditional choice for off-grid solar in Nigeria. They're based on lead-acid chemistry, are maintenance-free, and have been proven over decades.

### Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4)
LiFePO4 is the modern standard for solar storage. It offers dramatically better performance across almost every metric — at a higher upfront price that is rapidly becoming competitive.

---

## Head-to-Head Comparison

| Feature | AGM Gel | LiFePO4 (Lithium) |
|---|---|---|
| Usable capacity (DoD) | 50% | 80–90% |
| Cycle life | 300–500 cycles | 3,000–6,000 cycles |
| Weight (200Ah) | ~60kg | ~25kg |
| Charge efficiency | 75–85% | 95–99% |
| Temperature sensitivity | High — degrades fast in heat | Moderate |
| Self-discharge | ~5% / month | ~2% / month |
| Maintenance | None | None |
| Cost (200Ah) | ₦80,000 – ₦120,000 | ₦220,000 – ₦380,000 |
| Lifespan | 3–5 years | 8–15 years |

---

## Understanding Depth of Discharge (DoD)

This is the most important concept when comparing battery technologies.

**Gel at 50% DoD:** A 200Ah gel battery stores 200Ah of charge, but you should only use 100Ah to preserve battery life. Discharge deeper than 50% repeatedly, and you'll significantly reduce lifespan.

**Lithium at 90% DoD:** A 200Ah LFP battery can deliver 180Ah of usable power. This means a single 200Ah lithium battery effectively replaces nearly 2 × 200Ah gel batteries in real-world use.

---

## Total Cost of Ownership Over 10 Years

Let's compare 200Ah of usable capacity over 10 years (Enugu conditions, ~25°C average):

**Gel to deliver 200Ah usable capacity:** 2 × 200Ah gel = ₦200,000
- Replacement every 3–4 years: 2–3 replacement sets
- **10-year total: ₦400,000 – ₦600,000**

**Lithium to deliver 200Ah usable capacity:** 1 × 200Ah LFP = ₦300,000
- No replacement needed within 10 years
- **10-year total: ₦300,000**

> **The lithium battery is cheaper over time** — despite the higher upfront cost.

---

## When to Choose Gel

Gel batteries make sense if:

- **Budget is the primary constraint** — you need to minimise upfront cost
- **The system is temporary** — rental property or a short-term project
- **Replacement access is easy** — you're comfortable swapping batteries every few years

---

## When to Choose Lithium

Lithium is the right choice if:

- **You want the lowest total cost of ownership** over 8–10 years
- **Weight matters** — rooftop installations or mobile systems
- **High cycle count** — you discharge daily (as most solar systems do)
- **Temperature** — your battery location gets hot; lithium handles heat better
- **You don't want to think about batteries** — lithium is genuinely set-and-forget

---

## Nigerian Climate Considerations

Heat is the enemy of all batteries, but gel batteries suffer significantly more. Temperatures above 30°C accelerate sulphation in lead-acid cells, effectively cutting lifespan by 20–40% in hot environments like Lagos or Kano.

Lithium iron phosphate chemistry is far more stable at elevated temperatures. An LFP battery installed in a generator room or non-air-conditioned utility space in Enugu (average high: 34°C) will perform reliably for its rated lifespan.
