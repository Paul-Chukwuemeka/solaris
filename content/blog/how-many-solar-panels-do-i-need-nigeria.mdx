# How Many Solar Panels Do I Need in Nigeria?

One of the most common questions we receive is: *"How many solar panels do I actually need?"* The answer depends on three things — how much electricity you use, how much sun your location receives, and how many hours of backup you want. This guide walks through the calculation step by step.

---

## Step 1: Calculate Your Daily Energy Consumption

Start by listing every appliance you want to power and how long it runs per day.

| Appliance | Wattage | Hours/Day | Daily Wh |
|---|---|---|---|
| LED TV (43") | 80W | 6h | 480Wh |
| Refrigerator | 150W | 12h | 1,800Wh |
| Ceiling Fan | 75W | 8h | 600Wh |
| LED Bulbs (×8) | 80W | 6h | 480Wh |
| Phone Chargers (×4) | 40W | 4h | 160Wh |
| **Total** | | | **3,520Wh/day** |

Add a **25% efficiency buffer** (for inverter losses, wiring, and temperature derating):

**3,520Wh × 1.25 = 4,400Wh/day (total system requirement)**

---

## Step 2: Know Your Peak Sun Hours

Nigeria receives between **4.5 and 6 peak sun hours (PSH)** per day depending on location:

- **Enugu, Kano, Abuja:** ~5.0–5.5 PSH
- **Lagos, Port Harcourt:** ~4.5–5.0 PSH
- **Jos, Maiduguri:** ~5.5–6.0 PSH

For most calculations, use **5 PSH** as a conservative average across Nigeria.

---

## Step 3: Calculate Solar Array Size

**Solar Array (W) = Daily Energy Requirement ÷ Peak Sun Hours**

Using our example:

**4,400Wh ÷ 5h = 880W of solar panels**

With standard **500W monocrystalline panels**, you would need:

**880W ÷ 500W = 1.76 → round up to 2 panels**

> **Practical tip:** Always round up and add at least 10–15% more panel capacity than calculated. Dust, cloud cover, and seasonal variation will reduce real-world output.

---

## Step 4: Factor in Battery Autonomy

If you want to run at night or during cloudy days, you need battery storage. For 1 full night of autonomy on our example load:

**Battery capacity = 3,520Wh ÷ 0.8 (DoD) ÷ 12V = ~367Ah**

This means roughly **2 × 200Ah lithium batteries** (or 4 × 200Ah gel batteries at 50% DoD).

---

## Summary: How Many Panels for Common Nigerian Home Sizes

| Home Size | Typical Daily Load | Panels Needed | Battery |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 rooms (basic) | 2–5 kWh | 2–3 × 500W | 1–2 × 200Ah |
| 3–4 bedroom home | 8–15 kWh | 4–6 × 500W | 4–6 × 200Ah LFP |
| Small office | 15–25 kWh | 8–12 × 500W | 8–10 × 200Ah LFP |
| Restaurant/Retail | 25–50 kWh | 16–24 × 500W | Custom bank |
